Six words, one room

Many groups serving veterans and first responders in Phoenix work without knowing each other. ALLIES puts their leaders together, gives them practical training from working professionals, and leaves them with a list of partners they can call.

  • AAlliancesOrganizations that know each other and work together.
  • LLeadershipRun your group well, board to volunteers.
  • LLearningFormal nonprofit training in one morning.
  • IImpactKnow what your work changes and say it clearly.
  • EEmpowerLeave with tools you can use Monday.
  • SServiceKeep serving, with a stronger group behind you.
  • Veteran service organization posts
  • Unit support groups for loved ones
  • First-responder associations
  • Peer-support teams
  • New nonprofits started by someone who served

You do not need a degree or a board. You need an organization you want to make stronger.

Sponsor or give

ALLIES is free to attend. Money raised pays speaker honoraria, the working lunch and printed materials, and supports the Foundation's work with veteran and first-responder organizations. This is fundraising, and we say so plainly.

        Questions

        Is it really free?

        Yes. Attending costs nothing. Sponsors and donors cover the costs.

        Who should come?

        Leaders and volunteers of military, veteran and first-responder organizations, from brand new to well established. Bring a colleague from your group.

        Where is it?

        The City of Phoenix is providing the location. It will be announced with registration.

        Do I pick my breakout when I register?

        You choose a breakout on the day. Registration helps us plan room sizes and lunch.

        How do I get my confirmation?

        Eventbrite emails it to the address you register with. Check spam if you do not see it.

        Is ALLIES part of the City of Phoenix?

        No. The Foundation is a separate nonprofit. The City is providing the location.
